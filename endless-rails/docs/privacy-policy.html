ENDLESS RAILS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION] · Applies to the Android app “Endless Rails” (package com.endlessrails.game) and the web version of the game.

Developer / controller: [DEVELOPER NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS OR COUNTRY] · Contact: [CONTACT E-MAIL]

1. Summary

  • The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos, location or any other personal data.
  • Your settings, journey, lessons, achievements, statistics and unlocked extras are stored only on your device. The developer receives none of this data.
  • The Android version contains optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one in the “Extras” section. To deliver ads, Google processes some device data as described in section 3.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves its state in the app's local storage on your device: language, graphics and sound settings, control preferences, the current journey (position, train, weather, time of day), completed lessons and their best results, achievements, play statistics (distance, time, stops, stop accuracy), unlocked extras and a counter of rewarded videos watched today (used for the frequency limit). This data never leaves your device through the game. It is removed when you uninstall the app or use “Reset all progress” in the game. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this data in your Google device backup under your own Google account.

3. Advertising (Android version only)

The Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). At every app start the UMP SDK asks Google whether a consent message is required in your region. Where ads may be requested (consent given, or not required), the ads SDK is initialised and one rewarded video is pre-loaded in the background so that it is ready if you choose to watch it; nothing is shown unless you do. For this, and when you watch a video, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and diagnostics;
  • information about your interaction with ads (impressions, clicks, completion of a rewarded video).

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, to limit ad frequency and to prevent fraud and abuse, and — only where you have consented — to personalise ads. Data is transmitted encrypted. Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where required): before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings › Privacy › “Ad consent settings”. Ad content is limited to the “PG” rating.

The game itself does not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and the developer does not receive, sell or share personal data.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the optional ads, you can reset the advertising ID on the device and contact us.

5. Your rights

Because the developer does not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data should be made to Google (see links above); you can also contact us at the address above and we will help. Depending on your country (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

6. Changes

We will update this page if the game's data practices change, and change the “Last updated” date.
